# Privacy policy

*Note Organizer for Windows and for Android, made by Alexandra Moldovan. Last updated: 21 October
2026.*

Your notes stay on your device. Note Organizer has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and
no tracking. The Windows app connects to nothing, and the Android app does not even have
permission to use the internet.

Something leaves your device only when you send it - a note you share, a backup you save
somewhere else, or a message you write in the feedback form - or, on Android, when your phone's
own backup to your Google account is on. The only thing I ever receive is a feedback message,
with the internet (IP) address it was sent from.

## What the apps keep, and where

Everything you write - notes, the Trash (Settings → Recently deleted), templates, settings and
attached files - is kept in plain files on your device:

- **Windows:** a folder on your computer, `%APPDATA%\NoteOrganizer` for the installed app.
  Settings → About shows where it is. If the app ever fails, it writes what went wrong to
  `crash_log.txt` in the same folder; the log is never sent anywhere.
- **Android:** the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

The apps do not encrypt these files. They are protected by your device's own security: your
Windows account, or your phone's lock.

Your notes stay there until you delete them. Uninstalling the Android app deletes them from the
phone; uninstalling the Windows app leaves its folder, which you can delete yourself. Save a
backup first if you want to keep them.

## When something leaves your device

Only when you do it, or when your phone's own backup is on:

- **Sharing a note.** A note or a share code you send goes wherever you send it - a chat, an
  email - and that app's own privacy policy applies there. A share code is compressed text, not
  encryption: anyone who has it can read the note.
- **A backup or an exported note.** Saved where you choose: a folder, a USB stick, Google Drive.
- **Opening an attached file in another app (Android).** That app is allowed to read that one
  file, and nothing else of the app's.
- **Android's own backup.** If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android copies the
  app's data, notes included, to your Google account, and puts it back when the app is installed
  again. Google keeps that copy under its own privacy policy, and you can turn it off in the
  phone's settings. The app says so on its Settings → Backup page.
- **Send feedback**, described next.

## Send feedback

Settings → About → Send feedback opens a short form on this website, in your browser.

**What is sent:**

- what you write, and the kind of problem you pick;
- your email address, only if you give one, so that I can answer;
- a few details about the app that help find the problem: the app's version, its theme, how many
  notes and attached files you have, and the size of the notes file. On Windows, also the screen
  scale, whether Sharp text is on, the Windows version, and whether the app is installed or run
  from its source code;
- the internet (IP) address your message was sent from, which Netlify records with every message
  and uses in its spam check.

Never the title or the content of a note.

**When it is sent:** only when you press Send. The form lists the details before you do. They
travel in the part of the link after a `#`, which your browser does not send to the website.
(The Windows app's versions 0.95.382 to 0.95.391 put them in the link
itself, so they reached the website's host with the request for the page, as every web address
does.)

**How it is kept:** the form is handled by Netlify, the company that hosts this website, which
forwards each message to me by email. Netlify checks each message for spam with Akismet, a service
run by Automattic. Your message travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and is kept only in
my Netlify account and my mailbox, both protected by a strong password and two-step sign-in.

Netlify is in the United States. My mailbox is Gmail: Google keeps mail in data centres around
the world, the United States among them. Both take part in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework,
which the European Commission recognises as protecting personal data to the standard of EU law.
Akismet keeps data on servers around the world, and protects data it receives from Europe with
the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

I use a message only to answer you and to fix the problem. Apart from the companies named here,
it is never sold or shared with anyone. If your message leads to a fix, I may describe the
problem in my own words in the project's public list of issues on GitHub - never with your name,
your email address or your message itself.

I delete a message - from Netlify, its spam list included, and from my email, my answer
included - once it has been answered and its problem fixed, and at the latest 12 months after it
was sent. Akismet keeps most of what it checks for between two weeks and 90 days, as
[its privacy policy](https://akismet.com/privacy/) explains.

**Why I may keep it:** sending the form is your choice. I keep and use your message because I
have a legitimate interest in answering it and fixing what it reports, which is also why you
sent it (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). The same interest covers the spam check and the records Netlify
keeps of each visit: without them the site could not run, or would fill with spam. You can object
to this at any time, through the form, and I will delete your message.

## This website

- **Hosting.** The website is hosted by Netlify, in the United States. Like any web host, Netlify
  receives the technical details of each visit - your IP address, your browser, the page asked
  for. It handles them for me, only to deliver the page and to keep the site safe from abuse, and
  keeps them only as long as it needs them for that, as
  [its privacy policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/) explains. Netlify takes part in the
  EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
- **Nothing else.** No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, and nothing loaded from other
  websites.
- **What your browser remembers.** Two things, on your device only: the theme you picked for the
  website, and the demo's notebook - its sample notes, anything you write there, and its
  settings. Clearing this website's data in your browser removes both.

## Your rights

The only thing about you that I ever hold is a feedback message you sent, with the address it
came from. You can ask me for a copy of it, to correct it, to delete it sooner, to stop using it
while a question about it is settled, or to send it to you in a file you can take elsewhere; and
you can object to my keeping it. Ask through the
[feedback form](https://note-organizer-8f7772.netlify.app/feedback.html), with the email address
you sent your message from (or, if you gave none, a line of the message), so that I can find it.
I answer within a month.

If you think I have handled your data wrongly, you can complain to the data protection authority
in your country, or to Romania's, where I live: ANSPDCP,
[dataprotection.ro](https://www.dataprotection.ro).

## Children

The apps collect nothing from anyone, children included. Please don't send a child's personal
information through the feedback form.
