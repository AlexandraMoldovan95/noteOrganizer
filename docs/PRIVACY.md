# Privacy policy

*Note Organizer for Windows and for Android, made by Alexandra Moldovan. Last updated: 21 October
2026.*

Your notes stay on your device. Note Organizer has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and
no tracking. The Windows app connects to nothing, and the Android app does not even have
permission to use the internet.

Something leaves your device only when you send it - a note you share, or a backup you save
somewhere else - or, on Android, when your phone's own backup to your Google account is on. The
apps collect nothing, and send nothing to me.

## What the apps keep, and where

Everything you write - notes, the Trash (Settings → Recently deleted), templates, settings and
attached files - is kept in plain files on your device:

- **Windows:** a folder on your computer, `%APPDATA%\NoteOrganizer` for the installed app.
  Settings → About shows where it is. If the app ever fails, it writes what went wrong to
  `crash_log.txt` in the same folder; the log is never sent anywhere.
- **Android:** the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

The apps do not encrypt these files. They are protected by your device's own security: your
Windows account, or your phone's lock.

Your notes stay there until you delete them. Uninstalling the Android app deletes them from the
phone; uninstalling the Windows app leaves its folder, which you can delete yourself. Save a
backup first if you want to keep them.

## When something leaves your device

Only when you do it, or when your phone's own backup is on:

- **Sharing a note.** A note or a share code you send goes wherever you send it - a chat, an
  email - and that app's own privacy policy applies there. A share code is compressed text, not
  encryption: anyone who has it can read the note.
- **A backup or an exported note.** Saved where you choose: a folder, a USB stick, Google Drive.
- **Opening an attached file in another app (Android).** That app is allowed to read that one
  file, and nothing else of the app's.
- **Android's own backup.** If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android copies the
  app's data, notes included, to your Google account, and puts it back when the app is installed
  again. Google keeps that copy under its own privacy policy, and you can turn it off in the
  phone's settings. The app says so on its Settings → Backup page.
- **A page you open from the app.** Settings → About opens this policy, and, once the app is in
  a store, its page there for a review. Your browser or the store's app opens it as it would any
  other page; the app sends nothing with it.

## Feedback and reviews

To tell me that something is not working, or to share an idea, write a review on the app's page
in the Microsoft Store or on Google Play: Settings → About → Feedback takes you there, once the
app is published.

The store keeps your review under its own privacy policy, and shows it publicly with the name
you post under. I read reviews in the store's own pages for developers, which show me what the
store shows everyone - and on Google Play also your phone's model and Android version and the
app's version, so that I can find the problem. I answer in the store, publicly, as Note
Organizer. I keep nothing of a review outside the store, and you can change or delete your
review there at any time.

## If you write to me

The stores show the app's contact address, noteorganizer.app@gmail.com, and you can write to it
as well. If you do, I receive your message and your email address, and use them only to answer
you and to fix what you report.

My mailbox is Gmail: Google keeps mail in data centres around the world, the United States among
them, and takes part in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission
recognises as protecting personal data to the standard of EU law. The mailbox is protected by a
strong password and two-step sign-in, and mail reaches it over encrypted connections.

Apart from Google, which runs the mailbox, your message is never sold or shared with anyone. If
it leads to a fix, I may describe the problem in my own words in the project's public list of
issues on GitHub - never with your name, your email address or your message itself.

I delete a message, my answer included, once it has been answered and its problem fixed, and at
the latest 12 months after it was sent.

**Why I may keep it:** writing to me is your choice. I keep and use your message because I have
a legitimate interest in answering it and fixing what it reports, which is also why you sent it
(GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). You can object to this at any time, and I will delete your message.

## Where this policy and the website live

- **This policy** is published on Google Sites, a Google service; visiting it is covered by
  [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **The project's website,** which presents the app and has a demo, is hosted by Netlify, in the
  United States. Like any web host, Netlify receives the technical details of each visit - your
  IP address, your browser, the page asked for. It handles them for me, only to deliver the page
  and to keep the site safe from abuse, and keeps them only as long as it needs them for that, as
  [its privacy policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/) explains. Netlify takes part in the
  EU–US Data Privacy Framework. The website has no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no
  forms, and loads nothing from other websites.
- **What your browser remembers** of the website: two things, on your device only - the theme
  you picked for it, and the demo's notebook: its sample notes, anything you write there, and its
  settings. Clearing the website's data in your browser removes both.

## Your rights

The only thing about you that I may hold is a message you wrote to me. You can ask me for a copy
of it, to correct it, to delete it sooner, to stop using it while a question about it is
settled, or to send it to you in a file you can take elsewhere; and you can object to my keeping
it. Write to [noteorganizer.app@gmail.com](mailto:noteorganizer.app@gmail.com) from the address
you wrote from, so that I can find it. I answer within a month.

A review belongs to the store you wrote it in: you change or delete it there.

If you think I have handled your data wrongly, you can complain to the data protection authority
in your country, or to Romania's, where I live: ANSPDCP,
[dataprotection.ro](https://www.dataprotection.ro).

## Children

The apps collect nothing from anyone, children included. Please don't send a child's personal
information in an email or a review.
