Note Organizer

A notes app that keeps everything on your own computer: rich notes with tables and checklists, notes that repeat, and a calendar that shows them. No account, no cloud, no network calls.

Built by me, Alexandra Moldovan, with Claude Code, an AI coding agent. I decide what the app does, test it by hand and approve every change; the agent writes most of the code.

Try it in your browser

What it does

The everyday parts of a notebook, done carefully. The labels are the headings of the welcome note the app opens with.

Rich notes and tables

Bold, italic, colours, highlights, headings and lists, and tables you edit in place, the way a spreadsheet taught your hands to expect.

Write

A checklist under every note

With a count of what is done. The arrows that reorder items sit beside the boxes, away from the bin, so a slip of the mouse moves an item instead of deleting it.

Write

A calendar that shows your notes

Give a note a date and it appears on its day. Mark a day with a colour or an emoji, and open any day to see what is on it.

Plan

Notes that repeat

Daily, weekly, monthly or yearly, on the weekdays you choose, ending never, on a date or after a number of times. Any single day can still be edited on its own.

Plan

Search, sort and group

One search box looks through every note at once. Sort, show a single category, group by category, or arrange the list by hand.

Organise

Nothing lost to a slip

Deleted notes wait in Recently deleted. Backups move the whole notebook to another computer, and a share code copies a note as text into another copy of the app.
