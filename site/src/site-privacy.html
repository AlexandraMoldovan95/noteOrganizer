Privacy on this website

This website is mine — Alexandra Moldovan, in Romania. It presents Note Organizer and has a demo you can try in your browser.

It is hosted by Netlify, in the United States, which receives the technical details of each visit: your IP address, your browser and the page asked for. Netlify handles them for me, only to deliver the pages and keep the site safe from abuse, which is my legitimate interest (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), and keeps them only as long as it needs them for that, as its privacy policy explains. Netlify takes part in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission recognises as protecting personal data to the standard of EU law.

The site sets no cookies, has no analytics and no forms, and loads nothing from other websites. Your browser remembers two things, on your device only: the theme you pick for the site, and the demo's notebook — its sample notes, anything you write there, and its settings. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes both.

You can ask me about any of this, or object to it, at noteorganizer.app@gmail.com; and you can complain to a data protection authority, such as the one in the country where you live.
